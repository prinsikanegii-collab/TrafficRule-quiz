Important Traffic Rules

Red Signal

Stop before the stop line.

Yellow Signal

Prepare to stop and stop if it is safe.

Green Signal

Proceed only when the road is clear.

Wear a Helmet

Wear a properly fastened helmet while riding a two-wheeler.

Use Seat Belts

Always wear a seat belt while travelling in a car.

Follow Speed Limits

Always drive within the permitted speed limit.

Pedestrian Crossing

Slow down and allow pedestrians to cross safely.

Avoid Mobile Phones

Never use a mobile phone while driving.

Emergency Vehicles

Give way safely to emergency vehicles.
